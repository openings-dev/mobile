Find technology opportunities where communities already share them.

Openings brings public job listings from GitHub community repositories into one focused, mobile-first experience. Search open roles, narrow the results with useful filters, and always return to the original public source before applying.

WITH OPENINGS YOU CAN

• Discover technology jobs from public GitHub communities
• Filter by location, work model, seniority, technology, employment type, freshness, salary, and community
• Review job details, source information, and publication context
• Save interesting opportunities locally on your device
• Explore active communities and the authors behind public listings
• Use the app in English, Portuguese, Spanish, Italian, French, or German
• Choose light, dark, or system appearance

Openings is a discovery layer. It does not accept applications, verify employers, or guarantee that a listing is still available. Each opportunity links back to its original public source so you can confirm the details and continue there.

No account is required. Saved jobs and preferences stay on your device.
